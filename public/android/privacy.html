Keireki Craft for Android privacy policy

Last updated: [FECHA]

Who is responsible

David O. Contact: dquesada969x@proton.me.

Short version

Keireki Craft has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. Your CVs are created and stored on your phone. The developer does not receive them.

What the app handles

Everything you type or import into a CV: name, headline, contact details (email, phone, location, links), an optional photo, work experience, education, skills, languages, other sections, and your app settings (template, colours, language, backup choice).

To suggest the country code of an empty phone number, the app reads the country of the SIM card, of the mobile network and of the phone's language setting. This needs no permission, happens on the phone, and is used only for that suggestion: it is not stored apart from the phone number you save, and it is not sent anywhere.

Where it is stored

  • On your phone, in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it.
  • The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network.
  • Photos are chosen with the system photo picker; the app only receives the image you pick. A photo imported from a file that is not a valid image of reasonable size is dropped.
  • Files you export (PDF, JPG image, JSON) and the copy of all your CVs ("Back up all CVs") are saved where you choose or in Downloads, or shared with the app you choose. You can also copy the CV as an image to the clipboard. From then on they are under that app's or service's rules.
  • To share a file with another app, the app keeps it briefly in its private cache; those files are deleted after an hour.
  • Improve with AI (optional): when you tap it under a text and choose Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, the app copies a prompt with the text of that one field and asks your browser to open that assistant with the prompt in the link. Only then, and only that text: never your name, contact details or the rest of the CV. From there the assistant's own privacy policy applies (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google).

Android backup (optional, off until you choose)

On first launch the app asks whether to include your CVs, photos and settings in Android's backup.

  • If you accept: Android copies that data to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone, and also moves it to a new phone when you transfer your data from one to the other. Google stores and protects it under its own terms (Google Privacy Policy). The developer has no access to it. Your phone's system backup switch also has to be on.
  • Encrypted only: on Android 9 and later, the app hands its data to the cloud backup only when that backup is encrypted with your phone's screen lock (PIN, pattern or password). Without a screen lock no cloud copy is made. On Android 8 the system does not tell the app whether the backup is encrypted.
  • If you decline (the default): nothing leaves the phone.
  • You can change this at any time in the menu → Android backup. Turning it off replaces the earlier copy at the next automatic backup.

Sharing with third parties

None. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries. The only things that can reach a third party are the ones you send yourself: the text you send with Improve with AI to the assistant you choose, and the files you export or share.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays until you delete a CV, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the on-device data. A cloud backup you allowed can be deleted from your Google account (Google Drive → Backups).

Your rights

Because the developer holds no copy of your data, access, correction, export and deletion are done by you in the app (export, edit, delete). For a backup in your Google account, use your Google account settings. For any question, write to the contact above.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

Material changes will be published on this page with a new date and, if they affect the backup choice, asked again inside the app.
